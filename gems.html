How this works

When it refreshes. Every time you open this page it asks Hacker News again (it remembers the answer for 5 minutes, so moving a slider is quick). Tap Refresh to look again right now. The list is live: new posts show up as they're written, and a post drops off once it picks up more votes or comments than your limits, or falls outside the days you picked.

Since I last looked. This is the default. The page remembers when you last opened it (on the server, so your phone and computer agree) and looks back far enough to cover everything since: a day away is the last day, a long weekend is the last 3 or 4 days, at most 30. The first time, with nothing remembered, it shows 7 days. Reloading or tapping around within half an hour of opening counts as the same visit. Posts that arrived since your last visit are marked New, whichever window you pick.

Topics. Every post is filed under one or two of twelve topics, worked out from its words, so what you keep can be browsed and searched later. It's a good guess, not perfect. Search in Saved to ask "do I have anything for…?"

From my history. The Show HN posts you opened in your browsing history are in Saved too, tagged From my history and dated the day you opened them. Tap Unsave on any you don't want; they stop teaching it too. Pick the tag in Saved to see them together. Add a reason of your own to one and it counts as a full heart.

A post counts as overlooked when it has a lot of writing but few votes and comments. The overlooked score is its length divided by the attention it got, the same idea as pj4533.com/hn-overlooked.

For you puts posts that read like the ones you hearted first. It compares words, and your own reasons count extra. Posts from your browsing history count too, a little less than a heart until you give them a reason of your own. Each match shows the words and the post behind it, so you can see why it's there.

Idea from pj4533.com/hn-overlooked. Posts come from Hacker News through Algolia's public search.
